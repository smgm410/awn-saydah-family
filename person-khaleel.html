Antoon’s eldest brother that we know of: a teacher who left the ministry for business and became a respected New York merchant. He was a witness at Antoon’s wedding.

Born
Forzul. 19 March 1870 on his 1948 death certificate; March 1868 on the 1900 census, and about 1868 on the 1905 census (the 1910 census gives about 1867). These records disagree Probable
Came to America
1891 Confirmed
Work
Teacher, then merchant; the firm K. W. Saydah & Bros., Fifth Avenue Confirmed
Married
Katie (Kate), about 1902 (no marriage record seen; Katie, 22, and Victoria, 3, are in the 1905 census, and the 1910 census says Katie arrived about 1901) Probable
Died
26 November 1948 at 247 84th Street, aged 78; buried at Green-Wood Confirmed
Brothers and sisters
Antoon (Anton) Saydah, Michael W. Saydah, Murad Saydah, Sada Kirkish, Nustas Kirkish Confirmed

Spellings in the records: Khalil, Khaleel; the W. stands for Wahbeh, their father.
