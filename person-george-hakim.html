Virginia’s husband: a Columbia graduate and Army staff sergeant who later became a marketing executive in New Jersey. We list only what the records show.

Born
10 August 1918 (Social Security Death Index and Find a Grave). That the George who died in 1996 is Virginia’s husband rests on matching details Probable
Parents
Matilda Hakim of 561 47th Street, Brooklyn, in 1945; his father is not named Confirmed
Married
Virginia Saydah Hakim, around 6 May 1945, St. Nicholas Cathedral Confirmed
War and school
Staff Sergeant, U.S. Army, World War II; a Columbia College graduate Confirmed
Work
Account examiner at an advertising agency in 1950; later an executive vice president of marketing at Emerson Quiet Kool, Edison, New Jersey Probable
Children
Four (her obituary); two were at home in the 1950 census Family
Died
18 January 1996 (Social Security Death Index, Find a Grave, obituary index). Home given as Harrington Park (SSDI) or Tenafly (obituary index), New Jersey; buried at Brookside Cemetery, Englewood. A member of St. Anthony Orthodox Church, Bergenfield Probable
Sister
Eleanor, who was a bridesmaid at the 1945 wedding; the 1996 obituary index also lists a sister Eleanor Probable
Same man as the 1996 obituary?
Virginia’s 2015 obituary calls her husband George, also from Brooklyn, back from the war in 1945, and her church and burial place match his. No record yet names him in her obituary Probable
