Wassela’s father. The Syracuse Post-Standard says he spent his last eighteen months at the Clifton Springs health resort. Where he died is an open question, and he is probably buried in an unmarked grave at Oakwood Cemetery, Syracuse.

Born
About 1845 to 1850: March 1850 (1900 census), age 65 (1910 census), age 68 in the 1913 Arabic and Post-Standard notices, 1846 on Find a Grave Probable
Came to America
1892 Confirmed
Died
Saturday 11 October 1913. The Arabic notices say Clifton Springs; the state index says “Willard”, so the place is an open question Confirmed
Buried

Spouse

Another son

Spellings in the records: John Hodod (marriage certificate), Hanna Haddad (Arabic papers), John Hadad (census).
