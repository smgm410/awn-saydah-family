The two families began in different parts of Lebanon and met again in Brooklyn. These are simplified maps: positions are approximate and not to scale, and the coastline is only a rough guide.

Lebanon

  • Damour: The Aoun (Awn) family’s village
  • Beirut: Port city
  • Zahle: The Hadad family’s town (Wassela)
  • Forzul: The Saydah family’s village (position approximate)

The crossing

Antoon Saydah came to New York in 1898 (the ship and the exact arrival date are still in question: see Antoon arrives, 1898). Joseph and Assin’s family crossed in 1920 (The crossing, 1920). Anthony and Helen married in Brooklyn on 29 December 1940.

New York

  • Brooklyn: Where both families settled
  • Tuxedo Park: The 1916 crash
  • Tannersville: Summer 1945 in the mountains
  • Syracuse: Shehadi cousins; Hanna Hadad’s funeral
  • Clifton Springs: Where Hanna Hadad spent his last months, 1913

Not on this map: Allendale, South Carolina, where the Brooklyn Eagle says Antoon died on 17 November 1949 (one Arabic paper says Columbia, South Carolina).
