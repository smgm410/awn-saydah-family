Joseph took his wife and eight children from Damour to Brooklyn in 1920, when he was about fifty. He was retired by 1932.

Born
21 November 1869 is the birth date printed on his 1932 death certificate, but the age it gives (62 years 9 months 7 days) would put the birth about 7 December 1869. The certificate leaves the birthplace blank; Damour comes from the 1932 notice Probable
Died
14 September 1932, Brooklyn; buried at St. John’s Cemetery on 16 September Confirmed
Spouse
Assin Aoun Confirmed
Parents
Boutros (Peter) and Teresa Confirmed
Children
Nine: Nassib (Nessil) Aoun, Mathilde (Matilda) Gorayeb, John J. Awn, Theresa Koury, George Awn, Mitchell Awn, Elias (Louis) Awn, Anthony J. Awn and Esther Ayoub Confirmed
Came to America
1920, on the S.S. Grampian from Southampton by way of Quebec; crossed into New York State at Rouses Point on 5 September Confirmed

Spellings in the records: Joseph K. Owen (death certificate), Youseuf Aoun (1920 manifest), Joseph Khoury (Arabic papers; the K. stands for Khoury).
