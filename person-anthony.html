Helen’s husband. The youngest child to cross the ocean, he became a citizen in 1942 and worked in the family ladies’ wear business.

Born
6 March 1914, Damour Confirmed
Came to America
1920, aged six, as Antoine Aoun Confirmed
Citizen
10 November 1942 Confirmed
Married
Helen Saydah, Sunday 29 December 1940 Confirmed
Children
Five: Joseph, Jacqueline, Stephanie, Kathleen and Marie Confirmed
Work
Comptroller in a retail ladies’ wear business (1950 census). The family says he worked at Awn’s Specialty Shop Family
Died
Wednesday 6 July 1966, at home at 95 86th Street, Brooklyn, aged 52; buried at St. John’s Cemetery Confirmed
Parents
Joseph (Youseuf) Aoun and Assin Aoun Confirmed

Spellings in the records: Antoine Aoun (1920), Anthony Awn.
