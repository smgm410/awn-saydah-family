Born in Forzul, he reached New York in 1898 as a teenager, became an importer, and was a Mason. He was Helen’s father.

Born
15 August, in 1880 or 1881 (the day is the same in every record, the year is not), Forzul Confirmed
Came to America
October 1898, on La Touraine from Le Havre, by his own sworn statement Confirmed
Citizen
22 January 1914 Confirmed
Married
Wassela Hadad Saydah, 5 May 1912, Holy Trinity Church, Montague Street Confirmed
Children
Lily Saydah (1913), Helen Saydah (1917), Evelyn Saydah (1920) and Virginia Saydah Hakim (about 1921) Confirmed
Work
Importer in the linen trade; the Saydah Importing Company Confirmed
Died
Thursday 17 November 1949, in South Carolina; buried at Green-Wood Cemetery on 11 February 1950 Confirmed
Parents
Wahbeh Saydah and Noor Confirmed
Brothers and sisters
Khaleel W. Saydah, Michael W. Saydah, Murad Saydah, Sada Kirkish and Nustas Kirkish Confirmed

Spellings in the records: Anton, Antoun, Antoon; Saydah, Saidah, Sayda, Saida; Antoun Smyda on one 1898 manifest (not proven to be him).
