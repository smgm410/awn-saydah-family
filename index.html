This is the story of how the Aouns of Damour and the Saydahs of Forzul crossed an ocean, settled in Brooklyn, and became the family we are today. It is built from ship lists, census pages, naturalization papers, death certificates and newspaper clippings, and from the stories our family has told. Every record sits right beside the part of the story it belongs to. This is a first edition, not the last word, and it will keep growing as new records turn up.

A note for Mom, Aunt Jackie, Aunt Stephanie and Aunt Kathy

Much of what is in here began with something one of you remembered or kept, such as the memorial cards Aunt Jackie shared. How to read it

Every fact carries one of three labels:

  • Confirmed: a record shows it. Example: Anthony died on 6 July 1966, shown in his obituary (document 137).

  • Family: told to us, not yet found in a record. Example: the family name Aoun became Awn on arrival.

  • Probable: it fits the records but is not proven. Example: that Nassib is the right spelling of Nessil.

Names changed shape on the way. The family name Aoun became Awn (Family), and records also write it Owen and Aun. Saydah appears as Saidah and Sayda. These are the same families, and each time we say which spelling a record used.

What I would love from you: corrections, names for the faces in old photographs, and the stories no record keeps. Send Sarah a correction, a name or a memory and it will be added here, so this story keeps growing for the grandchildren and great-grandchildren.
