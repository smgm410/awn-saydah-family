Helen’s youngest sister. Her wedding to George M. Hakim was announced in May 1945. She died in 2015 in Harrington Park, New Jersey. We list only what the records and her obituary show.

Born
Her obituary says 29 June 1921. The 1930 census gives an age of 8 plus a fraction that is hard to read, so it does not settle the month; the 1925 census (age 4) points to a birth before June 1921. The obituary and the 1925 census are the records to compare Probable
Wedding announced
George Michael Hakim and Virginia, for Sunday 6 May 1945 at St. Nicholas Cathedral (the announcements of 2 May 1945) Confirmed
Married
That the wedding took place on 6 May 1945 is not yet shown by a marriage record; her 2015 obituary says they married in 1945 Probable
Later
The family was in New Jersey by 1962 (her obituary says 1961). She sang in the church choir at St. Anthony Orthodox Church, Bergenfield, and her obituary says she studied voice and piano at Juilliard Family
Children
Four Family
Died
26 August 2015, Harrington Park, New Jersey (obituary, The Record, 27 August 2015). Funeral at St. Anthony Orthodox Church, Bergenfield; burial planned at Brookside Cemetery, Englewood Confirmed
Parents
Antoon (Anton) Saydah and Wassela Hadad Saydah Confirmed
Sisters
Helen Saydah and Evelyn Saydah Confirmed
